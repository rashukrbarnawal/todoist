# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone involved in the Todoist Clone project will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being Respectful** - Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being Supportive** - Help fellow contributors and users
- **Being Professional** - Maintain professional conduct in all interactions
- **Being Open-Minded** - Accept constructive criticism
- **Being Patient** - Allow time for responses and discussions
- **Being Honest** - Report issues truthfully
- **Being Collaborative** - Work together towards common goals

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment** - Unwelcome attention, threats, or intimidation
- **Discrimination** - Based on identity, background, or beliefs
- **Abuse** - Verbal, written, or physical abuse
- **Spam** - Unsolicited or repetitive messages
- **Trolling** - Intentionally disrupting discussions
- **Doxxing** - Sharing private information without consent
- **Sexual Content** - Inappropriate sexual behavior or language
- **Violence** - Threats or advocacy of violence

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

1. Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
2. Taking appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior
3. Enforcing this code fairly and consistently
4. Protecting the privacy and safety of all members

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document** - Note the incident, date, and people involved
2. **Report** - Contact the project maintainers
3. **Provide Details** - Share what happened and any evidence
4. **Stay Safe** - Prioritize your safety and privacy

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment** - We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. **Investigation** - We will review and investigate the report
3. **Decision** - We will determine appropriate action
4. **Communication** - We will inform all parties of the outcome
5. **Resolution** - Implement actions to resolve the issue

### Consequences

Unacceptable behavior may result in:

- **Warning** - For minor infractions
- **Temporary Ban** - For repeated or serious violations (1 week - 3 months)
- **Permanent Ban** - For severe violations or repeated offenses
- **Legal Action** - For criminal behavior

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests)
- Official project events
- Project-related communications
- Unofficial spaces officially associated with the project
- When representing the project
